BUS DRIVER PLAYLIST - AUTO MUSIC VERSION

1. This folder needs Python 3 installed.
2. Put your MP3/audio files inside the "music" folder.
3. Double-click START_SERVER.bat.
4. Open http://localhost:8000 in Chrome.
5. Every audio file in music/ automatically appears in the playlist.
6. Add/remove songs from music/ and refresh the browser.

Only use audio you have permission to host/play.
